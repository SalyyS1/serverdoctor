| `F001` | warning | **-Xms and -Xmx differ** A heap that grows is a heap the collector keeps resizing, and the memory between the two numbers is reserved from the host either way. | `F003` | critical | **The watchdog is switched off** disable.watchdog stops Paper from dumping threads and ending a hung server.
